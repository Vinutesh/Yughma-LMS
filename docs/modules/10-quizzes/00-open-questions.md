# Open Questions — Quizzes

No blocking questions — narrowing applied, and one distinction worth stating explicitly:

- **Question types in v1: Multiple choice and True/False only** — both auto-gradable. Short answer, fill-in-the-blank, and matching ([MODULES.md](../../MODULES.md) §11) are deferred. This isn't just a smaller list — it's a deliberate product distinction: **Quizzes are auto-graded, objective checks; Assignments are the manually-graded, open-ended path.** Adding short answer to Quizzes would mean rebuilding [Assignments](../09-assignments/)' grading queue a second time for no real benefit — an instructor who wants open-ended graded work already has that tool.
- **Quizzes are created inside a course**, same as Assignments — a lesson type, not a separate authoring surface.
- **No separate Question Bank library screen in v1.** Questions are authored directly inside the quiz they belong to. A shared, reusable question bank (per [MODULES.md](../../MODULES.md) §11/§12, eventually shared with Assessments) is real value once there's a second consumer of it — building it for one consumer now is premature.
- **No CSV/QTI question import** — manual authoring only, matching "keep it simple."
