# Open Questions — Notifications

No blocking questions.

- **The bell + dropdown panel is already designed** ([Shell wireframes](../00-cross-cutting-shell/04-wireframes.md)) — this module adds the two things that weren't: a full **Notification Center** page (reached via "See all" in the panel, for anything older than the dropdown's short list) and **Notification Preferences** (in personal Settings).
- **Channels: in-app and email only** — push and Slack/Teams integrations stay deferred, per [MODULES.md](../../MODULES.md) §18.
- **Preferences are per category, not per individual notification type** — a handful of groups (Grading & Feedback, Deadlines, Course Updates, Team/Admin activity), each with independent in-app/email toggles — granular enough to be useful, not so granular it's a wall of switches.
